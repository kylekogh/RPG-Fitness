THE STATE OF THINGS
The city breathes, but only just. The old structures are cracking — institutions hollow, trust eroded, the comfortable certainties gone. It is not yet ruin. But ruin is close enough to smell.
THE BALANCE
The world does not tip on grand gestures. It tips on the small accumulation of choices made by people who could not see they were making them. A few more bad decisions and the fractures become breaks. A few more good ones and the city holds — not forever, but for another day.
THE HOPE
There are those who understand this. Who train not for glory but because a stronger self is a more capable one. Who cook rather than surrender to convenience. Who reach out, rest properly, keep learning. Who tend the small fires. These people are the reason the city is still standing.
You are one of them.
Or you are about to become one.
